## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/ObNTw8Uzwy6KQ/giphy.gif" width="30px">&nbsp;***About me***

I’m Daniel Lihm, a Tokyo-based Senior Software Engineer specializing in fintech and payment infrastructure. I have more than six years of experience building and integrating payment systems, including card processing, tokenization, recurring billing, digital wallets, 3D Secure, payment orchestration, and gateway integrations. I worked at KOMOJU from 2020 to 2024, where I focused on payment platform engineering, and later at Spreedly, where I worked on payment infrastructure and multi-gateway orchestration. I’m particularly interested in designing secure, reliable payment flows that handle real-world challenges such as retries, idempotency, stored credentials, PCI scope, wallet payments, and recurring transactions, and I’m comfortable taking payment integrations from architecture and sandbox testing through production readiness.
